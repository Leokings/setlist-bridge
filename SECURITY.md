# Security

The contract uses a pinned GenVM runner, bounded strings and collection sizes, explicit phases, role checks, closed AI enums, defensive response parsing, and independent validator replay.

Transition labels are creative judgments. The curator retains final ordering authority and the contract never books performers or handles money.

Do not submit secrets, private keys, personal data, copyrighted private material, or unsafe operational instructions. Wallet addresses are role selectors, not real-world identity proof. No funds are held or transferred.
